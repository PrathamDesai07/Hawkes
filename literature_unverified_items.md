## Items found beyond the list that do anticipate parts of 16–17 (these change the overlap table)

1. **Wei, Zhu, Zhang, Xie (AISTATS 2021, arXiv 2006.09439)**: KL pseudo-truth of a self-exciting process (piecewise-constant triggering), identifiability from concavity of the expected log-likelihood, a.s. QMLE convergence, sandwich covariance. Anticipates the *generic* content of Theorem 16 and Prop. 16.7 for batch estimation. Not: exponential dictionary with stability set, rate matching, constraint-binding criterion, active-constraint limit law, online, near-critical. → Problem 16 overlap risk **Medium**.
2. **Rong–Nair (arXiv 2602.20795, 2026)**: LS pseudo-true parameter on a prescribed basis, CLT under misspecification without mixing, numerics for P = 1…5 against a sum-of-exponentials truth; spectral-integral expressions. May anticipate Prop. 18.2 and Cor. 2(iv) (surrogate rate matching) and offers a route to the CLT item. Not: KL, online, stability proof, power law. → Problem 16/18 overlap risk **High** for the surrogate part.
3. **Spreij (1986, 1991)**: recursive (approximate-MLE) estimators for counting processes with parameter-dependent (linear) intensity, consistency and asymptotic normality via stochastic Lyapunov functions. Abstract level only (scans not readable). Anticipates the *well-specified* recursive consistency/CLT of Problem 17 in general form. → Problem 17 overlap risk **High** until read.
